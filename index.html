About VELORA

VELORA MENSWEAR is a men's fashion store in Bongalmora, North Lakhimpur. Our floor carries tees, oversized fits, shirts, denim, sneakers and caps, all on display so you can try before you buy.

This website is a browsing catalogue. Orders are requests sent over WhatsApp, and every purchase is completed in the shop after we confirm stock and price.

How to Order

  1. Browse
    Pick products and choose size and colour.
  2. Add to cart
    Review quantities in your cart.
  3. Send on WhatsApp
    Your request opens as a ready message.
  4. Collect in store
    We confirm stock and price. You pay and collect at the shop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay on the website?

No. This site has no payment system. You pay at the shop when you collect.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is not offered through this website. Orders are collected from the shop.

Is my order confirmed when I send the WhatsApp message?

No. It is an enquiry. The shop confirms stock and final price before anything is reserved.

Why do some items say "Price on request"?

Prices are not published for those items yet. Send an enquiry and we will reply with the price.

Can I visit and try items first?

Yes. Visit us in Bongalmora, near the Post Office, North Lakhimpur.
